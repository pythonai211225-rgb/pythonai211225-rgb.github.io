FROM THE MISSION LOG

Every expert was
once an explorer.

A little direction for your first expedition into AI.

01 Where should I start?

Start with NumPy and Pandas to learn how to work with data. Then explore linear regression before moving into neural networks.

02 How do the AI topics connect?

Machine learning discovers patterns in data. Neural networks are one family of machine learning models. CNNs specialize in spatial patterns, while RNNs work with sequences.

03 What can I build along the way?

Try a numerical data analysis, fit a prediction line, group observations with K-means, and explore the neural network lab. Each small experiment builds intuition for the next.
